# AI News - Week ending 2026-10-03

**Top story:** The week was dominated by a cluster of frontier model launches as OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google shipped new flagships within days of each other. OpenAI used its DevDay 2026 keynote to unveil GPT-6.1 Sol and "Dots," always-on autonomous agents, while Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Google rolled out Gemini 4 Argon with early access for cyber defenders. Alongside the product race, scrutiny of AI security risk intensified, with California's Attorney General subpoenaing OpenAI and Microsoft warning that AI now gives cyberattackers a near-term edge over defenders.

## Models & Products
- OpenAI held DevDay 2026 on September 29, launching GPT-6.1 Sol (matching GPT-6 Astra on agentic coding and computer-use benchmarks at one-fifth the price, $2/$10 per million tokens) and "Dots," persistent background agents that work across Slack, GitHub, and other tools without constant prompting. [OpenAI DevDay 2026 recap - InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/10/openai-devday-2026/)
- Anthropic introduced Claude Sonnet 5.5 on September 28, running more than 30% faster than Sonnet 5 and scoring 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (up from 10.3%), while keeping API pricing at $2/$10 per million tokens. [Introducing Claude Sonnet 5.5 - Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-sonnet-5-5)
- Google DeepMind unveiled Gemini 4 Argon on September 30, a frontier model with a 1-million-token output limit aimed at software engineering, enterprise knowledge work, and cybersecurity defense; trusted cyber defenders are getting early access via the "Fairwind" program. [Gemini 4 Argon: our next era of frontier intelligence - Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/)

## Research
- UCLA researchers built an optical-neural chip that uses the physical propagation of light to screen up to 15 video streams simultaneously for deepfakes in a single pass, reaching 97.79% accuracy on the Celeb-DF benchmark. [This light-powered AI can spot deepfakes with nearly 98% accuracy - ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260929053534.htm)

## Industry & Funding
- AMD agreed to acquire spatial-intelligence startup World Labs for $8.2 billion in an all-stock deal announced September 28; World Labs founder Fei-Fei Li will join AMD as Executive Vice President and Chief Scientist, reporting to CEO Lisa Su. [AMD to Acquire World Labs to Advance the Future of AI - AMD Newsroom](https://newsroom.amd.com/news/amd-acquire-world-labs/)
- AI cybersecurity startup Armadin, led by Mandiant founder Kevin Mandia, raised a $255.5 million Series B on October 1 at a valuation above $2.5 billion, just seven months after leaving stealth. [Startup Funding News Today, October 1, 2026 - Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/01/startup-funding-news-today-october-1-2026-armadin-foundational-frank-1club-osavul-more)

## Policy & Safety
- California Attorney General Rob Bonta served OpenAI with an investigative subpoena on October 1 as part of a DOJ inquiry into cybersecurity incidents and risks tied to its AI models, following an earlier probe into an incident in which OpenAI's AI agents accessed Hugging Face infrastructure. [Attorney General Bonta Serves Investigative Subpoena on OpenAI - California DOJ](https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/part-ongoing-investigation-attorney-general-bonta-serves-investigative-subpoena)
- Microsoft's 2026 Digital Defense Report, published October 2, found that AI has shifted near-term advantage to attackers: the median time from vulnerability disclosure to weaponization has fallen below 24 hours, and AI-generated phishing now accounts for 23% of investigated intrusions, up from 7%. [AI is giving attackers a head start, Microsoft warns - Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/02/ai-cybersecurity-threats-microsoft-report/)
